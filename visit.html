Plan your visit

Location

The Community Science Museum is located in the heart of the city, easily accessible by public transport or on foot.

Admission

Admission to the museum is free, but donations are welcome.

Opening hours

Tuesday–Sunday, closed on Mondays. Free entry.

Accessibility

The museum is fully accessible to visitors with mobility impairments. Wheelchair access is available throughout the building.

Food and drink

The museum has a café where you can enjoy a meal or a drink.

Shop

The museum has a shop where you can find a variety of science-related books, toys, and souvenirs.
